Seoul police · October 26, 2026

One man, one AI agent, 90 organizations hacked

A 32-year-old with no IT degree started handing his attacks to AI in late August. About 90 organizations were breached and 8.2 million records stolen before Seoul police arrested seven people.

8.2M records stolen60 companies in 44 days7 arrested

Agentic Insider / Tools / AI hacking case
By the Agentic Insider desk · Oct 30, 2026 · Sources: JoongAng Daily

What happened

  • About 90 organizations hit: hospitals, universities, golf courses, cram schools and affiliates of large companies
  • 8.2 million records stolen, including personal data and internal documents
  • About 60 companies fell in 44 days (Aug 31 to Oct 13) once AI tools joined in, per local reports
  • 38 organizations got extortion emails, 360 million won (about $272,000) demanded in total; no payment has been confirmed
  • Helpers were recruited through a Telegram ad for 'simple phone calls for pay'
  • Police say an in-house AI analysis system helped them catch all seven within four months; it is South Korea's first large AI-assisted hacking arrest

How to protect yourself

  1. Use a different password on every site, kept in a password manager.
  2. Turn on two-factor authentication for email, banking and social accounts first.
  3. Check whether your email shows up in known breaches at haveibeenpwned.com.
  4. Treat any 'pay or we leak your data' email or call as a crime: report it, don't pay.
